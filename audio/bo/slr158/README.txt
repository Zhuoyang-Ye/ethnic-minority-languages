NICT-Tib1 / OpenSLR SLR158 pilot audio

This directory is intentionally a small pilot subset, not the complete
33.5-hour corpus. The app expects the two files named in data/bo/slr158.js:

  b1-1-greeting.wav
  b1-1-wellbeing.wav

The two WAV files are the selected utterances 007_01_007_24 and
012_01_012_337 from the SLR158 release. Do not copy the full archive into this
repository.
